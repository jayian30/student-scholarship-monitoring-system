Student Scholarship Monitoring and Academic Compliance System

A centralized, role-based platform administering academic summary submissions, document verification, policy compliance evaluations, and scholarship renewal decisions for Merit and Community Scholars.

Access System Portal Run Functional Tests (TC01–TC10)

Merit Scholar Program

Merit

Designed for top-tier academic achievers maintaining strict excellence standards without subject failures or dropped courses.

  • Max Permitted GWA: 1.75 (Lower is better)
  • Min Enrolled Units: 18 units
  • Max Failed Subjects: 0
  • Max Incomplete Grades: 0
  • Max Dropped Subjects: 0
  • Deadline: Oct 30, 2026, 5:00 PM Manila Time

Community Scholar Program

Community

Designed for scholars engaged in civic and community service with flexible unit load and one permitted dropped subject.

  • Max Permitted GWA: 2.25 (Lower is better)
  • Min Enrolled Units: 15 units
  • Max Failed Subjects: 0
  • Max Incomplete Grades: 0
  • Max Dropped Subjects: 1
  • Deadline: Oct 30, 2026, 5:00 PM Manila Time

User Roles & Workflow Architecture

Scholar Role

Scholars can log in securely, view their assigned scholarship program, submit semester academic summaries with official grade document references, monitor verification status, and resubmit returned applications with corrected data.

Scholar Access

Scholarship Staff Role

Staff members oversee the verification queue, substantiate grade evidence, trigger the automated compliance evaluation engine, record explicit renewal decisions (Approved or Held), and review system audit history.
